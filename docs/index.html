    Lookup server

    PCGS and Greysheet keys live on your own small lookup server (see README), never in this app. Leave blank to use scan + manual entry only.

    PCGS lookups

    Turn this off to scan a pile of slabs quickly, then use “Fill in missing details” on the Collection tab when you have lookups to spare. Coins you've looked up before never use another lookup.

    Scanning tips

    • Fill the guide box with the barcode. If it looks blurry, back up and use the Zoom slider instead of moving closer.
    • Tilt the slab slightly so the plastic doesn't reflect light straight back at the camera.
    • Still stuck? Tap Photo: the iPhone camera focuses better, and the app reads the barcode from the picture.
    • PCGS: barcode or QR on the front label. NGC: barcode on the back of the label, or the QR code.
    • No signal? Type the cert number instead and the coin is still saved.
